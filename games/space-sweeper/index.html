Arcade

Space Sweeper

Clean up space, build a better rocket and fly further than ever!

🔩Space junk: fly close to scoop it up ☄️Meteorites: dodge them, they knock off a shield 🌍Blue planets: land and walk around to find 3 rocket parts. They build onto your rocket and send you to the next level! 🪐Planets and suns pull you into orbit. You're safe there. Push away, or tap BOOST to break free of a sun 🚀Each level your rocket gets bigger, but space gets emptier. At level 5, find the red planet...

Touch: drag anywhere to fly, tap BOOST. On a planet, tap ◀ ▶ to walk and FLY to take off. Computer: arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space to boost, P to pause.
