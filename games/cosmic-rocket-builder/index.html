Cosmic Rocket Builder

Build a rocket from parts and launch it into space! Clever builds fly further: the Moon, Mars, other stars, even other galaxies. Silly builds go POOF!

Tap a part, then tap the grid to build. Tap a part on the grid again to take it off.
Computer: number keys pick parts, arrow keys and Space build, L launches.
