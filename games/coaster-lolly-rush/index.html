Lolly Rush

You're in the front seat! Lollies and broccoli float over the track ahead.

Speed up to grab lollies before they vanish.
Brake to let the broccoli disappear or swing past before you reach it.
Catch a love heart to gain a life (up to 5).
Run out of lives and the ride is over.

Keyboard: Up / W = go, Down / S / Space = brake, P = pause
Touch: hold right side = go, left side = brake
