Privacy

This site is a collection of free games made by kids. We keep it simple and safe.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No personal data. We don't ask for or collect names, emails, photos or locations.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies. The games don't send anything anywhere.
  • Saved scores stay on your device. Some games remember your best score or sound setting in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes it. We never see it.
  • No chat. Players can't message each other.

If that ever changes (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated first.
